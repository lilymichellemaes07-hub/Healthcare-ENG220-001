## Abstract
-Access to healthcare is important for maintaining a healthy community, but the people living in rural areas can face challenges when trying to receive medical care. This project will focus on healthcare access in Cuba, New Mexico. Although Cuba does not have a full service hospital, the area has three health centers that provide important services to the community. These services include primary care, behavioral health, dental care, an onsite pharmacy, and enabling services. Enabling services can help connect or refer patients to larger and better equipped hospitals when they need medical care that cannot be provided locally.
	The goal of this project is to examine how accessible healthcare is for people living in Cuba and what challenges still exist despite the services currently available. The project will use healthcare services, insurance coverage, income, transportation to medical facilities data to help better understand the situation in Cuba. These datasets are suitable because they can help show where the gaps in healthcare access may exist. The expected outcome is to identify these gaps and better understand what improvements could make healthcare more accessible for residents of Cuba, New Mexico. One of these ideas being an app/website that can allow people to have more connection to pharmacies and better care, as well as virtual doctor visits.
